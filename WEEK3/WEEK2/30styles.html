AMRITA VISHWA VIDYAPEETHAM

    Mata Amritanandamayi, globally revered as "Amma," is a spiritual leader 
and humanitarian from Kerala, India, known as "The Hugging Saint" for 
having embraced over 40 million people. Through her organization, 
Embracing the World, she manages a vast network of charitable projects, 
including Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham and Amrita Hospitals. Her 
philosophy centers on selfless love and compassion, transcending 
religious and cultural boundaries to provide global disaster relief 
and social welfare.
