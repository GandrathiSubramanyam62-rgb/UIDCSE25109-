HIGHEST FANBASE IN IPL


1. CHENNAI SUPER KINGS

Chennai Super Kings** is one of the most popular teams in the Indian Premier League. The team represents Chennai and is known for its strong performance and loyal fans. Under the leadership of MS Dhoni, CSK has won several IPL titles and built a reputation for consistency and teamwork. Because of their yellow jerseys and passionate supporters, CSK fans are proudly called the **Yellow Army**. The team is admired for its spirit, discipline, and exciting cricket.


2. MUMBAI INDIANS

**Mumbai Indians** is one of the most successful teams in the Indian Premier League. The team represents the city of Mumbai and is known for its strong players and excellent performance. Under the captaincy of Rohit Sharma, Mumbai Indians have won multiple IPL titles. The team has a huge fan base and is admired for its teamwork, talent, and exciting matches.


3. ROYAL CHALLENGERS BENGALURU

Royal Challengers Bengaluru** is a popular team in the Indian Premier League. The team represents the city of Bengaluru. RCB is well known for its passionate fans and strong players like Virat Kohli. Even though the team has faced many tough seasons, it remains one of the most loved teams in the IPL because of its fighting spirit and exciting matches.
